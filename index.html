What is ribattseke.co.za?

This site is a one-stop platform for South African electricians and trade students — built and run by a practising electrician and registered assessor. It brings together everything you need to pass your trade test, stay compliant, and grow your practice.

  • LMS (Learning Management System) — structured study sessions on SANS 10142-1, EIR 1992, Construction Regulations 2003, SANS 10198-1/2, SANS 10292, SANS 1973-3, and SANS 10313 Lightning Protection, each ending in a timed quiz.
  • Services — CoC issuance, solar PV installation, distribution board upgrades, training panel construction, NAMB trade test prep, and more.
  • Study Packs & Tools — curated SANS and reference downloads, professional electricians toolkits, and stationery combos — everything a student needs on the bench.
  • Projects — a portfolio of completed electrical and renewable energy installations for reference and credibility.
